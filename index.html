Personal Learning Experiments

Run simple within-person exercises to examine calibration and retrieval. These activities are educational demonstrations; differences do not establish causality.

Confidence Calibration Lab

Estimate your confidence before revealing the answer. Your prediction is compared with your result. This is a practice demonstration, not a validated psychological assessment.

Practice & Reflect

Try recording a prediction before studying, testing yourself later, and reflecting on whether your confidence matched your performance.

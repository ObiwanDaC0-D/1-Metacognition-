Metacognition

Understand your thinking. Monitor your learning. Improve your strategies. Build intellectual independence.

Explore the science of thinking about thinking

Metacognition encompasses knowledge about cognition, monitoring of understanding, and control of learning strategies. Explore competing models, test your confidence, and practice evidence-informed techniques.
